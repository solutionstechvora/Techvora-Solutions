IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
1. Replace every instance of hello@techvora.lk with your actual business email. It is a placeholder, not a verified address.
2. Replace sample products and LKR prices with real stock, accurate specifications and current prices.
3. Add your phone/WhatsApp, shop address, opening hours, delivery, return/refund and privacy policies.
4. The cart is a front-end demo. It does not save orders, check stock, calculate delivery or collect payments. It prepares an email enquiry only.
5. To accept real online payments and manage orders, connect an ecommerce backend such as WordPress + WooCommerce and a payment provider available to your business in Sri Lanka. Enable HTTPS/SSL and backups.
6. Upload index.html and the entire assets folder together. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
